# DISCORD_SERVER.md

## Overview

This document defines the official structure, guidelines, backlog, and growth strategy for the the **Trophi.gg Discord Server**.  
It is designed as a **hybrid document**: part documentation, part executable backlog to be used by whoever sets up and maintains the server.

---

## 1. Purpose of the Server (Pre-Launch → Post-Launch)

The Trophi.gg Discord server exists to:

- Build an early community around achievement hunters, trophy hunters, and completionists.  
- Serve as the main hub for announcements, roadmap updates, and changelogs.  
- Collect feedback on features, UX, bugs, and future ideas.  
- Provide a place where players can share achievements and celebrate mastery together.  
- Enable AI-powered achievement help and discussion (initially manual, later via bot).  
- Support events, challenges, and future programs (Trials, Legendary runs, etc.).  
- Connect players, creators, and the Trophi team in one central place.

This server should feel like **the home base for people who want to master their games.**

---

## 2. Core Principles

1. **Clarity first** – Channels should be intuitive, minimal, and clearly labeled.  
2. **Scalability** – The structure must support future expansions (more channels, events, roles) without needing a full redesign.  
3. **Identity-driven** – Roles, emojis, and copy should reinforce the Trophi “mastery, completion, progress” identity.  
4. **Safe, respectful space** – Strong moderation to keep the environment welcoming, inclusive, and fun.  
5. **Growth-ready** – The server should be ready to onboard users coming from the landing page, email, and future in-app links.  
6. **Low friction** – Easy to navigate, easy to participate, easy to understand where to post.  

---

## 3. Information Architecture (Server Structure)

This is the **initial structure**, focused on Pre-Launch and MVP.  
Additional categories/channels can be added in V1/V2.

### 3.1 Categories & Channels

#### 🧭 Category: `WELCOME`

- `#welcome` – First touchpoint, brief intro and quick-start.
- `#rules` – Server rules and code of conduct.
- `#roles` – Reaction roles to personalize experience.

#### 📢 Category: `ANNOUNCEMENTS`

- `#changelog` – Product updates, new features, bug fixes.
- `#roadmap` – Roadmap snapshots, phase changes (MVP → V1 → V2).
- `#news` – General announcements, early access waves, events, trials.

#### 🎮 Category: `COMMUNITY`

- `#general-chat` – Main community discussion.
- `#show-your-achievements` – Screenshots, trophy pops, completion screens.
- `#game-discussions` – Talk about specific games, series, genres.
- `#setup-gallery` – Share gaming setups, rigs, and desk flex.
- `#off-topic` – Anything outside games & Trophi.

#### 🤖 Category: `AI HELP` (Soft mode initially)

These channels will exist **from Pre-Launch**, but AI automation will evolve over time.

- `#achievement-help` – Ask questions about achievements and how to obtain them.  
- `#walkthroughs` – Discuss routes, runs, and spoiler-safe guidance.  
- `#build-consulting` – Help with builds for RPGs or complex games.

> **Pre-Launch / MVP:** these channels can be answered manually (by you + mods + external tools).  
> **Later:** they will be powered by the Trophi Discord Bot.

#### 🧰 Category: `SUPPORT`

- `#bug-report` – Issues in Trophi (product or site).
- `#feature-requests` – Ideas for new features or improvements.
- `#feedback` – General feedback, impressions, suggestions.

#### 🔒 (Optional, internal) Category: `STAFF`

- `#staff-chat` – Internal communication between admins / mods.
- `#mod-log` – Manual logs, decisions, notes.
- `#auto-log` – Logs from bots (joins, bans, automod events).

## 6. Future-Proofing & Best Practices

To make it easy to expand later, follow these guidelines from the start:

- Use clear, lowercase channel names with hyphens (e.g., `#game-discussions`, not `#GameDiscussions`).  
- Use channel topics/descriptions to explain what belongs in each place.  
- Avoid creating too many channels before there is demand. Start lean.  
- Use slowmode in `#general-chat` if traffic becomes hard to moderate.  
- Keep `AI HELP` channels open but manage expectations until the bot is live (pin a message clarifying current status).  
- Keep internal/staff channels clearly separated and hidden from normal members.  
- Use roles thoughtfully: don’t create a new role for every small detail.
